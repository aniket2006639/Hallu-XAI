The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France. It was completed in 1889 for the World's Fair and stands about 330 metres tall.

Mount Everest, in the Himalayas on the border between Nepal and China, is Earth's highest mountain above sea level at 8,849 metres.

The speed of light in a vacuum is exactly 299,792,458 metres per second.

Python is a programming language created by Guido van Rossum and first released in 1991.

At standard atmospheric pressure at sea level, water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.

The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest of Earth's oceans.

Marie Curie won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1903 and the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 1911. She was born in Warsaw, Poland.

Albert Einstein published his theory of general relativity in 1915.

The capital city of Australia is Canberra, not Sydney or Melbourne.

The human heart has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles.
